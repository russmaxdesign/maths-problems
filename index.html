Order of priority:

    BODMAS, BIDMAS, BEDMAS and PEMDAS

    These are four names for the same rule. Orders, Indices and Exponents all mean powers and roots. Parentheses is another word for brackets. Which one you were taught mostly depends on where you went to school: BODMAS and BIDMAS in the UK, Australia and India, BEDMAS in Canada and New Zealand, PEMDAS in the United States.

    Name First Second Third Fourth
    BODMAS Brackets Orders Division and Multiplication Addition and Subtraction
    BIDMAS Brackets Indices Division and Multiplication Addition and Subtraction
    BEDMAS Brackets Exponents Division and Multiplication Addition and Subtraction
    PEMDAS Parentheses Exponents Multiplication and Division Addition and Subtraction

    The letter order in the third column is misleading in every version. Division and Multiplication sit on the same level and are worked left to right, whichever letter comes first in the name. Addition and Subtraction work the same way. PEMDAS putting M before D does not mean multiplication is done first.
